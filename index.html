About

Our story

Printed sewing patterns, designed with care and delivered to your door.

Z-PAHUA began as a small pattern studio dedicated to one idea: that a well-made sewing pattern should be easy to follow and satisfying to sew from, start to finish.

We design and print sewing patterns for garments across women's, men's, and kids' wear. Every order is printed on A4 paper, numbered and tiled for easy assembly, and physically delivered to your address — no printer or download required on your end.

"A pattern is a promise: that the person holding it can trust each step and end up with something they love to wear."

Support

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, and returns.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders arrive within 3–7 business days. You'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

What is your return policy?

Since every pattern is printed specifically for your order, we can't accept returns for a change of mind — but if your pattern arrives damaged or misprinted, we'll reprint and redeliver it free within 30 days. See our Returns page for details.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we ship within the country only. International shipping is on our roadmap.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and visit My Orders to see the status of any order you've placed.

What payment methods do you accept?

Right now we accept Cash on Delivery only. Card payments are coming soon.

Still have a question?

Our small team is happy to help with anything from sizing to delivery.

How delivery works

Once you place an order, we print your pattern on A4 paper, tile and number the pages, and pack it for delivery. Orders are processed within 1–2 business days, and standard delivery takes 3–7 business days depending on your location. Orders over $50 ship free.

  1. We print — your pattern is tiled and numbered on A4 sheets.
  2. We pack — a layout guide and instructions are included.
  3. We deliver — most locations within 3–7 business days.

Payment

We currently accept Cash on Delivery only — you pay in person when your pattern is delivered. Card payments are coming soon.

Our returns policy

Because every pattern is printed specifically for your order, we're not able to accept returns simply for a change of mind. If your pattern arrives damaged, misprinted, or isn't what you ordered, let us know within 30 days of delivery and we'll reprint and redeliver it at no extra cost.

How to request a reprint

Visit our Contact page with your order number and a photo of the issue, and we'll take it from there.
